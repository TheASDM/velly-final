A press floor, a short cellar, a tasting counter, and a room off the back where the owner lives. [Lotan](/en/Venturia/Characters/PCs/lotan) holds the lease, the living space included, and sells wines and meads in volume to taverns across the tiers under the [Wine Merchants' Guild](/en/Venturia/Factions/wine-merchants-guild). The building has no working frontage on the canal side — which suits its owner.


---

## The House Rule
Lotan does not lend and does not borrow. Anyone who needs coin is offered work on the press floor instead, and paid fairly for it. A rack of coins from a dozen ports is nailed to a ceiling beam; regulars add to it.
