# The Overlook

**District:** Fog boundary &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Mood:** Watchfires, grey light, held breath &nbsp;|&nbsp; *"Where the city stops looking away."*

The Overlook is the high edge of Venturia where the streets end and [Vallombrosa](/en/Venturia/Locations/vallombrosa) begins. From its cliff terraces, the fog line is not a rumor or a story. It is the view.

This is where the Fog Wardens keep watch.

---

## Character

The Overlook is quieter than the rest of the city. People lower their voices here without being told. Some come to stare into the fog, some to leave offerings, and some because they have nowhere else to take a question the city cannot answer.

For the Fog Wardens, it is a workplace: logs, patrols, signal lamps, tired eyes, and rules written by generations of people who learned what not to do.
