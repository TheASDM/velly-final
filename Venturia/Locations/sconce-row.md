A quiet row of narrow houses over shopfronts, three streets from the Gardens' lower gate. One of them — two rooms over a shopfront shuttered for years — is still held in [Valentro](/en/Venturia/Characters/PCs/valentro)'s name. He visits rarely and never stays long.
