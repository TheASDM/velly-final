# The Cask & Cube

![The Cask & Cube](/images/locations/the-cask-cube.png)

## Overview

The Cask & Cube is the physical space where the Valley of Shadows campaign is played. Located in Dustin's home, it began as a spare bedroom, but in November 2025, upon completion of The Last Wrongs campaign, a flooring project evolved into a comprehensive gaming environment.

## The Space

The room features:
- DMX-controlled stage-quality lighting
- In-ceiling speakers
- Wainscoting and cedar ceiling beams
- Hand-built notice board and dartboard surround
- Custom gaming infrastructure

## History

Construction began in November 2025. The first session held in the completed space was **The Last Toll** in May of 2026, a one-shot that took place on the eve of what would later be called The Great Reunion.
