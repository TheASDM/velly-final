Two flights of external ironwork lead to a shuttered window and a low room over the Amaranth's north scene dock, with a view across the theater's roofline to the canal. The company uses it as dead storage: crates of retired prompt-books, scene stock nobody has needed in years, and dust. Children who grow up around the theater have always known ways up that the company forgets.
