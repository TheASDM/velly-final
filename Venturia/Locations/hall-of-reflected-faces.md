# The Hall of Reflected Faces

**Type:** Private gallery &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Keeper:** [The Veil Court](/en/Venturia/Factions/veil-court) &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Collection:** Three centuries of Masquerade masks

The Hall of Reflected Faces is one of the Veil Court's most exclusive spaces: a gallery holding a mask from every Autumn Masquerade for nearly three hundred years.

It is an archive, a shrine, and a social weapon depending on who is invited to stand inside it.

---

## Character

Each mask preserves a version of the city that chose to show itself for one autumn. Some are famous. Some are politically embarrassing. Some are remembered only because the Court refused to let them vanish.
