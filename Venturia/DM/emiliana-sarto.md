# Professor Emiliana Sarto

> **DM-ONLY - CAMPAIGN SPOILERS.** Players should not read it.

**Type:** VAVA faculty &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Pressure role:** The Obstructor &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Want:** Validation

Professor Emiliana Sarto is the lone VAVA professor with a long-standing Vallombrosa obsession. She has studied the boundary, the fog, and Venturia's fey-touched quirks for fifteen years, mostly while being politely ignored.

Now the Leaked are happening and TL walks out of the fog. For the first time, she looks like the expert in the room.

---

## Genuine Want

Emiliana wants to be taken seriously, to have her work validated, and to document TL's knowledge properly. She is right that Vallombrosa research matters. Her mistake is tying her credibility to TL's credibility.

---

## Collision With The Party

The party's attempts to question TL look to Emiliana like more people trying to silence the one person who proves she was right. She can use academic politics, public advocacy, and her growing platform to shield him.

If the party proves TL is Silas, Emiliana can become a fierce ally because she will be furious at being used.
