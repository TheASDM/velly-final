# The Verdigris Family — DM Notes

> **⚠ DM-ONLY — CAMPAIGN SPOILERS.** Players should not read it.

**Status:** In development — jenred_'s one-shot, with Dustin's blessing &nbsp;|&nbsp; *"Mirrors that lie, and the people who buy them."*

The antagonist layer beneath the Verdigris's respectable surface. The public family page shows only the honest mirror-crafters; everything below is the part they'd never admit to. Much of this is still being shaped, so treat it as a working draft rather than locked canon.

---

## The Undertow Supply
The Verdigris supply the specialty mirrors used in **[The Undertow's](/en/Venturia/Locations/the-undertow)** memory-trading rituals — glass in which a memory can be watched being extracted, reflected in the surface, before it's taken. The Undertow pays extremely well for them.

---

## The Memory Pattern
A recent, quiet pattern: people who received Verdigris "custom reflection mirrors" later developed memory problems — gaps in recollection, decisions they couldn't explain.

---

## Isidore's Ambition
Isidore wanted to supply the Undertow with mirrors that show the owner what they *want* to see rather than what *is* — deceptive glass, a step beyond the family's true-mirror reputation. It's part of his broader push to expand the operation.

---

## Thalia's Experiments
Thalia has begun experimenting with mirrors that show what *was* — glimpses of past reflections. Cosimo is terrified she'll attract the wrong kind of attention. Per jenred_, both Thalia and Cosimo are intended to function as antagonists in the one-shot (each motivated by their own goals, with consequences following naturally — Dustin's preferred way of writing them).

---

## Cosimo's Leverage
Cosimo uses Noname's fiancé to further his own ends, feeding him information gleaned from the mirrors to remove rivals and personal enemies. Nothing is stated outright as illegal — per Dustin, corruption and illegality are simply the unspoken norm in Venturia, and everyone behaves as though they aren't.

---

## Open / In-Development Threads
- **A spoiled, conceited Verdigris obsessed with Noname's fiancé** — who never returned the feeling, to the family's quiet embarrassment ("not good enough," and they don't want it known she's obsessed). jenred_ floated either reshaping Thalia into this character or adding a new sister; **unresolved.**
- **A "sister steals one of her sister's mirrors" beat** jenred_ wants to write in — tied to the obsession thread above.
- **Cosimo's survival:** Dustin's preference is that Cosimo survives the one-shot if possible — "but if the dude dies, the dude dies."
- **Consequences:** jenred_ noted the Verdigris don't face real consequences for their actions *within* this one-shot.
- **"Noname's fiancé"** here is the one-shot's version, with his Undertow ties deliberately kept vague; reconcile against campaign canon (**[Maruk Grommarg](/en/Venturia/Characters-NPCs/maruk-grommarg)**) before linking the two.
