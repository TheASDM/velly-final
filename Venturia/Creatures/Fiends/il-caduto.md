## Monstrous and Dreadful

They are driven mostly by their hatred and an urge to kill. They plot their movements beforehand and attempt to strike at the hearts of their enemies with their overwhelming power. They look normal most times but when they reveal their true shape, it is usually the last sight that any mortal witnesses.
