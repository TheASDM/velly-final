## Unnoticed

These fiends are almost-human. Their flesh, body, eyes, mind, and everything is the same as a human. They are in their own body, no fiend possessed them, but they cease to be humans anymore. They lost their souls, gave up on their breath, or some other vile deed transformed what they are into mere fiends.

## Silently Dangerous

They are not worse than a human with evil intentions, but they hide what they truly are and await their time for malice. Even adept spellcasters cannot easily identify these fiends. Thus, they can be anywhere, hiding in plain sight—smiling at your face or shaking your hand.

## Mark for Greater Evil

Their most dangerous threat is to leave a mark for a bigger and more dangerous hunter. They silently and subtly mark a creature they desire, and the marked ones usually become the targets of more dangerous devils, evildoers, and deceivers.
