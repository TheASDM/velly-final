## Breathing Fire

They are what remain of sacrificed souls who were thrown into the fires of endless torment and who clawed their way out through sheer hatred. They look for revenge and retribution, and if they cannot find it they take out their malic on whatever may fall in their path. Appearing as a skeleton or zombie with cracks in their body filled with red hot flame or white fire traces, they are a sight not easily forgotten. They can control fire, and are immune to it as most fiends are.

## Servants of Evil

They are usually conjured by cultists as a means to terrorize their enemies, inflict fear upon them, or as a punishment for a fellow cultist, an adversary, or a rival to condemn their soul for eternity. It is also possible for humanoids filled with hatred and fury to rise as Il Rosso after they suffer a deadly injury during a fire.
