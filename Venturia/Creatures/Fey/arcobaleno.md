## Shining after Rainy Days

The rainbow fairies are creatures that are seen as glimpses of hope, especially on islands, along coastlines, and between tides. They are believed to appear after rainbows, as their name suggests, and they bring joy lost after a rain. They bring tiny but nutritious berries to travelers and often help those in distress find their way.

## Colorful Personalities

The rainbow fairies are loud, friendly, and come in very different personalities, depending on the color they choose to wear. They accompany adventurers, travel distant seas, explore dangerous areas, get married, and delight in song and wine, depending on the light that shines on them when they wake up.

## Magical Protection

They can grant magical protection to their allies for a brief period of time, as they are used to the perils of adventuring and other threats on the magical side of the world. It is said in legends that the rainbow fairies are born within the magic of rainbows, so they are able to protect others as well.
