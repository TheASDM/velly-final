## Trickster

The vivo usually excels at creating illusions and temporarily creating actual materials on a whim. They live in glades, coastal areas, and mostly away from human eyes. However, their extremely curious nature occasionally makes them visit human settlements and maybe even dwell in cities.

## Different Sizes

The vivo are usually Small, but they can turn themselves into Medium or Tiny sized as well, to help them hide better. They sometimes join seafaring adventures disguised as humans or tiny creatures to avoid the eyes and help the crew or make their lives miserable.
