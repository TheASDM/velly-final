## Pure Energy

The celestiali are a type of ethereal creature that usually comes from the realm of undead, the spirits stuck in the material realms for too long. However, instead of terrorizing innocents as horrifying undead, these souls found their way to the pure magic of the world and became the manifestations of innocence and deeds that are considered good.

## No Memories

Although they remember almost nothing about their past lives, the celestiali usually have a singular goal they want to follow: like protect a certain church, escorting pilgrims to a holy place, guiding seafarers to safety, punishing pirates who kidnap people from coasts. Some are rather furious and vengeful, while some can be charming, peaceful and even defenseless.

## Cycle of Nature

Some of the celestiali are transformed into more beastly shapes rather than humanoid forms, they can appear as a guiding deer in the forest, a loyal dog for a person in pain, and many other variable conditions, shapes and interesting aspects, sometimes even as a plant.

## Hidden One

Hidden ones resemble a beast of their choosing, usually elk, antelope, or a similar animal of good omens and rarely take the form of predators. They usually exist to protect or guide ones in need, lost creatures, distressed travelers, or similar people.
