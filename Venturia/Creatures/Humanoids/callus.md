## Callus

The lord of Breakwater rules from the deck of his flagship, rapier in hand and a grin that never reaches his eyes. He has carved his reputation from the bones of rivals and the hulls of merchant vessels. When cornered, he fights like a cornered animal — beautiful, precise, and utterly without mercy.
