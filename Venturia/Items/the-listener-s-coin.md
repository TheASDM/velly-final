# The Listener's Coin

![The Listener's Coin](/images/items/the-listener-s-coin.png)

{IMAGE}

## Description

A bronze coin worn smooth by long handling, its faces too weathered to identify any nation of origin. Despite its age and apparent ordinariness, the coin is suffused with subtle magic.

## Function

When pressed flat against any solid surface—a wall, door, floor, or window—the coin grants the holder perfect auditory access to the space beyond. Conversations at normal volume carry clearly. Whispered words arrive with full clarity. Footsteps, breathing, and ambient sound all transmit as though the holder stood in the room itself.

The effect lasts exactly ten minutes, provided the coin remains in contact with the surface throughout. Breaking contact ends the listening immediately.

The coin has 1 charge and recharges at dawn each day.

## Known History

The coin was previously owned by **Aurelio** before passing to its current holder, **Roxy**.
