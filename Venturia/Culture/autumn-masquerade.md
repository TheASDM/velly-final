The Autumn Masquerade is Venturia's most important social event: a citywide season of masks, bargains, performances, courtships, public spectacle, private meetings, and identities worn with absolute commitment.

Going unmasked during Masquerade is considered deeply unlucky. Going half-heartedly masked can be worse.

---

## What Happens

Everyone wears elaborate masks. Major social connections are forged, business deals are sealed, feuds are paused or sharpened, and theater runs constantly across every district. The city becomes a continuous performance in which the line between role and self matters less than outsiders expect.

The final night culminates in a staging of **[The Tragedy of Vallombrosa](/en/Venturia/Culture/tragedy-of-vallombrosa)**, whose plot changes from year to year depending on which version of the legend the chosen company decides to perform.

---

## Why It Matters

The Masquerade is not treated as costume play. In Venturia, a mask can be courtesy, confession, aspiration, defiance, or protection. During autumn, those meanings grow louder. A person who commits fully to a face may find the city more willing to believe them.
