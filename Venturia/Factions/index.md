# Factions

- **[The Abbey of Saint Viro and the Faithful](/en/Venturia/Factions/abbey-of-saint-viro)** — The city's foremost religious institution. Runs hospitals, orphanages, and food relief.
- **[The Binding Crafts Guild](/en/Venturia/Factions/binding-crafts-guild)** — Carpenters, masons, shipwrights, and the building trades.
- **[The Covenant of Silver Scales](/en/Venturia/Factions/covenant-silver-scales)** — The premier merchant association for high-value agreements. Contract law and arbitration.
- **[The Fog Wardens and the Shadowed Wardens](/en/Venturia/Factions/fog-wardens)** — Venturia's law and order, and the specialist division that handles what the visible Watch isn't equipped for.
- **[The Gardener's Guild](/en/Venturia/Factions/gardeners-guild)** — The horticultural guild that tends the Tiered Gardens.
- **[The Harbor Wardens' Compact](/en/Venturia/Factions/harbor-wardens)** — The guild controlling everything moving through the ports.
- **[The Metals Guild](/en/Venturia/Factions/metals-guild)** — Smiths, jewelers, metalworkers, and the Mirrorwrights.
- **[The Veil Court](/en/Venturia/Factions/veil-court)** — The semi-secret society that keeps the city's traditions of masks, identity, and performance, and stages the [Autumn Masquerade](/en/Venturia/Culture/autumn-masquerade).
- **[Volante & Associates](/en/Venturia/Factions/volante-associates)** — Aurelio Volante's spice-and-dye house in the Market Tiers.
- **[The Warehouse Consortium](/en/Venturia/Factions/warehouse-consortium)** — Constanza Marea's dockside storage and cargo power base.
- **[The Wine Merchants' Guild](/en/Venturia/Factions/wine-merchants-guild)** — The guild behind the Winecradle and Venturia's impossible vintages.
