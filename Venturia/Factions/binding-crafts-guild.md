# The Binding Crafts Guild

**Type:** Guild &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Trades:** Carpenters, masons, shipwrights, construction workers &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Council seat:** [Master Artisan Celeste Orion](/en/Venturia/Characters/NPCs/celeste-orion)

The Binding Crafts Guild speaks for the trades that physically hold Venturia together: timber, stone, rope, scaffolds, piers, repairs, foundations, and the hands that know when a wall is lying.

Its members work across every tier of the city, but its politics are closest to the Market Tiers and the working districts.

---

## What They Do

They build, repair, inspect, and argue over the city's material reality. In a place of canals, terraces, old foundations, and buildings added to buildings for centuries, that work is never simple.
