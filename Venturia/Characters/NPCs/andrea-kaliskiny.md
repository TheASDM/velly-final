A druid of the **Fifth Order of Celtans**, and an ancestor of **[Lotan](/en/Venturia/Characters/PCs/lotan)**. As with everything in **[House Kaligor](/en/Venturia/Lore/house-kaligor)**, this is Lotan's telling.

---

## The Knowledge

The party returned to Barovia carrying pieces gathered across Faerûn and no idea how to combine them. Andrea met them there. She was the one who held the knowledge and the ability to bring the items together and make the seal — without her the collection was a pile of relics.

Lotan is explicit that she was another follower of the same nameless patron **[Velonar](/en/Venturia/Characters/NPCs/velonar-kaligor)** served, sent to assist. He is equally explicit that the party did not know this, and that she was destined to become Velonar's wife — something, he says, that possibly only the patron knew.

He delivers this as a romantic flourish. It reads differently written down: a power arranged a marriage across a generation and told nobody in it.

---

## After

She married Velonar, bore his children, and with him bought the Wizard of the Wines. Lotan counts the greats on his fingers when he gets to her and gives up partway.
