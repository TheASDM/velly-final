Noname is a Fog Warden — twenty-four, built for rage and trying to master it. She came up at [Liminal House](/en/Venturia/Locations/liminal-house), the home for the city's orphans and its fog children, after her parents died young; her own father had been one of those fog children, a person who walked out of [Vallombrosa](/en/Venturia/Locations/vallombrosa) as a child with no past to speak of. Now she's a patient at [St. Viro's Respite](/en/Venturia/Locations/viros-respite), not for a wound anyone can see, but because her memory of the night her fiancé vanished has gone soft and grey at the edges — and she means to get it back.


---

## Concept
A Barbarian Fog Warden working to calm her rage. Raised at [Liminal House](/en/Venturia/Locations/liminal-house) after her parents' early deaths.


---

## Family
Her father was a fog child — one of those who appeared out of [Vallombrosa](/en/Venturia/Locations/vallombrosa) as a child, memoryless, and was raised by Liminal House. He was lost to the fog he had spent his career watching. Her mother, also a Warden, declined in the years after and died at [St. Viro's Respite](/en/Venturia/Locations/viros-respite) — treated for "continued decline of mental and physical health," gone of causes the record leaves unspecified. When Noname asks what happened to either of them, she gets redirections. She followed her parents' path into Liminal House, and then into the Wardens.


---

## What Brought Her to Viro's
Her fiancé, a fellow Fog Warden named [Maruk Grommarg](/en/Venturia/Characters/NPCs/maruk-grommarg), disappeared under circumstances she can't fully reconstruct. Her memory of that night is foggy and unclear — and that fog is the reason she's now at [St. Viro's Respite](/en/Venturia/Locations/viros-respite), hunting for what she's missing.
