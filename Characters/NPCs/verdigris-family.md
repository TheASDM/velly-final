# The Verdigris Family

**Type:** Noble crafting family &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Guild:** the Mirrorwrights &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Quarter:** the Market Tiers &nbsp;|&nbsp; *"Quietly wealthy from something nobody thinks about."*

<!-- DM NOTE (not rendered): Antagonist/secret material on this family lives at /en/Venturia/DM/verdigris-family. Keep this public page surface-only. New, player-developed (jenred_) — not yet in the main knowledge base. -->

The family that has controlled the Mirrorwrights' guild for five generations. Old money, not flashy — quietly wealthy from owning something everyone needs and nobody thinks about. They craft mirrors, and in a city where reflections don't always behave, they understand Venturia's mirror-strangeness better than almost anyone.

---

## The Guild
The Verdigris run the Mirrorwright's Court in the Market Tiers, where most of what they make is ordinary looking-glass and a precious few are "true mirrors" that reflect honestly despite the city's peculiarities. Their word carries weight in any matter touching mirrors, reflection, or identity.

## The Family
- **[Cosimo Verdigris](/en/Venturia/Characters-NPCs/cosimo-verdigris)** — Patriarch and respected guild elder, in his seventies.
- **[Isidore Verdigris](/en/Venturia/Characters-NPCs/isidore-verdigris)** — The heir and a master craftsman, in his forties.
